Proof Before Next | Native 3D

Engine: Three.js perspective camera and WebGL-rendered 3D world, independent from the aerial photo map. Thumb joystick handles 360-degree speed/direction; drag anywhere on the right side to look.

World: OSM/OpenFreeMap mapped road and building geometry converted into original 3D mesh roads, houses, porches, trees, storefront forms, sidewalks and lighting. Individual facades are interpretive, not exact replicas.

Still unverified: performance, model appearance, accurate placement of each house and movement/collision on your actual tablet.

Not built: finished character art, vehicles with enter/exit animations, traffic, full physics and missions.
