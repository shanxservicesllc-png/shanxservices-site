HOMEWOOD.
ALIVE.

Explore a stylized 3D micro-neighborhood inspired by Homewood, Pittsburgh 15208. Walk the blocks, see residents chatting, shoppers outside local-style stores, traffic passing, and an occasional community street gathering. Enter the blue coupe and drive. Original fictional people and buildings, no copied Street View imagery.

Touch: left joystick to move, drag the open screen to turn your view, CAR to enter or exit, RUN for speed, ↻ to recenter. Keyboard: WASD, mouse drag, E, Shift, R.

Street names are authentic local references, but intersections and buildings are an artistic prototype, not an exact real-world map. Google Maps is for street-level visual research.

V2 ambient-scene proof, not the approved final visual quality. Unlisted URL, not password-protected or monetized. Requires an internet connection for Three.js.
