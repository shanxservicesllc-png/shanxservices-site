HOMEWOOD.
AFTER HOURS.

Explore a fictional neighborhood inspired by Homewood, Pittsburgh. Walk around, turn the camera, approach a street car, get behind the wheel, and drive through the block. This build uses actual imported animated 3D character and vehicle models rather than sphere-built people and box cars.

On phone: left joystick to walk or steer. Drag an empty part of the scene to turn the view. Tap ENTER CAR near a parked car; tap again to exit. Hold RUN to sprint or brake. Recenter the camera at any time. Keyboard: WASD, Shift, E, R, V.

This is a new playable technical build, not a claim of San Andreas-level art quality. Temporary demonstration assets are credited in project notes. Real neighborhood cartography and original final characters are later production stages.
